The first tagged release of Lyrics Synced with Music: the words to whatever you are playing, in the Omarchy bar, highlighted line by line in time with the song. It picks the track up from your media player, so there is nothing to search for and no account to make.

**Install** with `omarchy plugin add https://github.com/crmne/omarchy-lyrics.git --enable --yes`, then `omarchy bar move crmne.lyrics --section right --before omarchy.tray`.

## New

- **Lyrics that follow the song.** When a track has synced lyrics, the line being sung is highlighted and the panel scrolls along with it. Lyrics without timestamps are scrolled to roughly where the track is. By @crmne.
- **Nothing to search for.** The plugin reads the track from any player that speaks MPRIS and looks it up on [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net), trying an exact match on artist, title, album and length before widening to a search. Track length decides between candidates, so a live version does not get the studio recording's timing. By @crmne.
- **Click a line to jump there.** Every line seeks the player to that moment. Scrolling by hand stops the panel following, and the crosshair button picks the song back up. By @crmne.
- **A timing nudge for uploads that run early or late**, a text size you can step up or down, and an expanded panel whose size you set in the bar settings. All three are remembered. By @crmne.
- **The cover, title and artist in the panel header**, and a bar icon that hides when nothing is playing. By @crmne.
- **Keyboard and script control.** `omarchy-shell crmne.lyrics toggle` opens and closes the panel, with `show`, `hide`, `refresh` and `status` beside it, ready to bind in Hyprland. By @crmne.
- **Careful with what the network sends.** Lyrics are rendered as plain text, the fetch helper only follows `https` on `lrclib.net`, and responses are size-limited before and after decompression. Lookups are cached for a month under `~/.cache/omarchy/lyrics`. By @crmne.

## Known limitations

- Lyrics come from LRCLIB only. A track it does not have shows as having no lyrics, and the timing is only as good as the upload (hence the nudge).
- Lyrics without timestamps cannot be followed line by line, only scrolled to an estimate.
- Needs Omarchy Quattro with its Quickshell-based shell, and `/usr/bin/python3` for the fetch helper.
