Lyrics now turn up for songs whose title carries a credit in brackets, such as a composer or the film a song is from. Nothing changes in how the panel looks or works.

**Update** with `omarchy plugin update crmne.lyrics`, or install with `omarchy plugin add https://github.com/crmne/omarchy-lyrics.git --enable --yes`.

## Fixed

- **Songs with a credit in brackets in their title are found.** Players often add something to a title that is not its name, like `Song (M. Jayachandran)`, and LRCLIB's search looks for every word, so it found nothing and the panel said there were no lyrics. When a search comes back empty, the plugin now searches once more without the bracketed words, still by the same artist and still matched on length. It does not when the player reports no artist, since a title that short is shared by too many other songs. The lookup still goes to LRCLIB and nowhere else. By @crmne, from the retry in [#1](https://github.com/crmne/omarchy-lyrics/pull/1) by @akashanil-dev.
