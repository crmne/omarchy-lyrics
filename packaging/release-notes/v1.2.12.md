Lyrics are now looked up only when you open the panel, and the album-art helper connects only to public internet addresses. Both come from the marketplace review. The panel looks and works as before.

**Update** with `omarchy plugin update crmne.lyrics`, or install with `omarchy plugin add https://github.com/crmne/omarchy-lyrics.git --enable --yes`.

## Fixed

- **What you play reaches LRCLIB only when you open the panel.** Every track change used to send its artist, title, album and length to LRCLIB, even with the panel closed. Now nothing is sent until you open the panel, which then looks the track up; a track looked up before still shows at once from the cache. A middle-click refresh while the panel is closed happens the next time it opens. By @crmne; thanks [HANCORE-linux](https://github.com/HANCORE-linux) for the report.
- **A player can no longer make the shell reach services on your machine or network.** The player chooses the cover URL, so opening the panel could fetch from anything reachable over HTTPS, such as a local service. The album-art helper now connects only to public internet addresses: loopback, private, link-local and carrier-grade NAT addresses are refused before any connection is made, on every redirect hop, and the address checked is the one connected to. By @crmne.

## Known limitations

- Opening the panel for a track not looked up before now takes a moment while the lyrics are found, and the bar icon lights up for a track only once the panel has looked it up.
- Covers served from your own machine or local network no longer show, and covers are not fetched through a proxy set in the environment.
